How is completion calculated?

Declared completion = recorded “verified” contracts / non-cancelled declared contracts. In-progress, blocked, ready, candidate and deferred work remain in the denominator. This is not independent verification or an estimate of remaining engineering effort. An invalid or missing ledger yields UNKNOWN.
